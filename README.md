# Store-Annual-Report
MS Excel dashboard (Power Pivot, ETL)

INSIGHTS:

Channel: Amazon dominates order flow, but Flipkart and Myntra are competitive. Hence, they need great platforms for campaign optimisation.

Region :Maharashtra leads, but Karnataka and UP show promising return, thus need to consider expanding inventory or localised marketing in these areas.

Customer: Adults and teenage women are the highest purchasing groups, thus they are ideal for fashion-focused campaigns.
Women drive the majority of purchases—hence can target promotions accordingly.

Additional: High fulfilment rate(92 % delivered) reflects solid logistics and customer satisfaction.
