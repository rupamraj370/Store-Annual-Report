# Store-Annual-Report
MS Excel dashboard (Power Pivot, ETL)
INSIGHTS:
Fashion-focused SKUs suggest a strong affinity in ethnic and formal wear—Hence, track product-level conversions for deeper insights.
Amazon dominates order flow, but Flipkart and Myntra are competitive. Hence, they need great platforms for campaign optimisation.
Maharashtra leads, but Karnataka and UP show promising returns—Thus need to consider expanding inventory or localised marketing.
Adults and teenage women are your highest purchasing groups—Thus they are ideal for fashion-focused campaigns.
Women drive the majority of purchases—hence can target promotions accordingly.
High fulfilment rate(92 % delivered) reflects solid logistics and customer satisfaction.
